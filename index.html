Professional Profile

Muh. Fitrah is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics Education at Universitas Muhammadiyah Bima, Indonesia, and is currently pursuing a doctoral degree in Educational Research and Evaluation at the Graduate School of Universitas Negeri Yogyakarta.

His work aims to strengthen educational theory, advance research methodology, foster instructional innovation, and promote evidence-informed educational practice.
